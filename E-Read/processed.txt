Press RB and LB to scroll. Press A to process book.txt. This will edit the text so it works better. Press Left/Right to change charechters per line and Up/Down to change how many lines can show at a time. Also please note that the Thumby Color's RAM is 512 KB so if your book.txt file is around there you may run out of RAM. Lastly, press the MENU button to exit the E-Reader. This will save your current postion in the text.
