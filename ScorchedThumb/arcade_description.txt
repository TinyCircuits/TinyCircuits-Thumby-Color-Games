Scorched Thumb

Classic tank artillery on a tiny screen. Set your angle and power, read the wind, and lob shells across a destructible battlefield until one tank is left standing.

- 2-4 players hotseat, any of them can be a CPU (Easy, Medium or Hard)
- Destructible, scrolling battlefield twice the width of the screen
- 9 weapons including MIRV, funky bomb, roller, dirt bomb and napalm
- Shop between rounds, plus parachutes, shields and fuel for driving
- Target Practice: 12 levels to earn stars on
- Hall of Fame for game and round wins
- Aim Help shows the start of your shot's path (handy for younger players)

Controls:
Left/Right: aim
Up/Down: power
A: fire
B (tap): change weapon
B (hold) + Left/Right: look around the battlefield
LB/RB: drive (needs fuel)
MENU: pause

A tribute to Scorched Earth (1991) by Wendell Hicken. No original assets are used; the sounds are the author's own.
